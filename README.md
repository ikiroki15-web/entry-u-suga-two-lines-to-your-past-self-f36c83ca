## What it does

`main.ts` writes exactly two lines of text to `/output/entry.txt`:

1. A warning to the writer's past self about one specific moment, in a single sentence.
2. One sentence on why that moment mattered and what changed because of it.

The text names a concrete moment — a time, a place, a decision, and a sentence said out loud — and the second line traces the cause and effect from that moment onward.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Requires Node 22.18 or later. No dependencies, no network, no input files: the output is deterministic.

## The entry

> When Dad says 'sell the house now' at the kitchen table on Christmas Eve 2019, don't nod along.
>
> That nod sold the only home your mother ever owned, and the rent you have paid since has cost you the deposit on a place of your own.
